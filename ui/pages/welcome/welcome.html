    01 — HELLO

    Hi. I'm Mochi.

    Welcome to Burrow — a calm, quick browser with a few tricks. Let's set it up together. It takes about a minute.

    PRESS ENTER OR → TO CONTINUE

    02 — BRING

    Bring your stuff.

    Moving from another browser? Bring your bookmarks and history along. They're only read and stay on this computer.

    Looking for other browsers…

    Passwords and sign-ins stay where they are — you'll sign in to each site once.

    03 — LOOK

    Make it yours.

    Pick light or dark, and a colour. Every other colour in Burrow is made from this one.

    04 — SEARCH

    Where should searches go?

    When you type something that isn't a question or an address, Burrow searches here.

    05 — AI

    Your AI, one keypress away.

    Type a question anywhere and Burrow sends it to your AI — or keep it docked beside the page with Ctrl Shift A. Sign in once and you stay signed in.

    Sign in opens the service's own page in a tab. You sign in on their site — Burrow never sees your password.
